# Egab

> Empowering journalists in the Global South to publish nuanced stories in international media

## Overview

Egab is a journalism platform founded in late 2020 that connects local journalists from underrepresented regions with international media outlets. The platform specializes in solutions journalism - reporting on how people respond to problems in their communities.

## Vision

To build a world where the news is the trusted source for accurate and holistic representation of events and communities in the Global South.

## Mission

To combat parachute, superficial journalism by enabling local journalists across the Global South to provide nuanced coverage that defies stereotypes about their communities.

## Strategy

To become the go-to-platform for any journalist wishing to kickstart their international journalism career and to be the go-to-platform for any media outlet seeking to cover news of the Global South.

## Core Services

### For Journalists
- Access to a large network of international news organizations from their hometown
- Mentoring and guidance throughout the story creation process
- Fair payment advice and compensation for published work
- Skills development and editorial support

### For Media Outlets
- Access to diverse contributors and stories from the Middle East, Africa, and Global South
- Cost-efficient reporting without flying in reporters or paying for accommodation/fixers
- High-quality, exclusive stories from local perspectives
- Meeting diversity and inclusion goals with authentic representation

## Focus Areas

- **Geographic**: Middle East, Africa, and broader Global South (60+ countries)
- **Journalism Type**: Solutions journalism
- **Topics**: Community responses to challenges, underreported stories, Palestinian voices, environmental action, social innovation

## Key Information

- **Founded**: Late 2020
- **Headquarters**: Cairo, Egypt
- **Website**: https://www.egab.co/
- **Social Media**: @egab.co (Instagram), @Egab_co (X/Twitter)
- **Impact**: Supported local journalists in over 60 countries to publish in top media outlets

## Quality Standards

Egab ensures story quality through:
- Comprehensive mentoring from pitch to publication
- Editorial guidance and feedback
- Focus on engaging, high-quality pieces
- Emphasis on authentic local perspectives

## Recognition

The platform's work has been recognized by several awards, with particular pride in helping local journalists and media outlets tell inspiring stories for global audiences.
